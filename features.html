Pairs

Understand your differences.
Find a way forward.

See where your working styles align and differ. Then agree on how to work together.

  1. Start with understanding.

    A pair read brings your working styles into view, side by side.

    For colleagues in the same workspace. Both complete the core read and agree before a pair read is written. Opening a read is recorded for both people.

  2. Build an agreement.

    Answer 25 questions each about how you work together. Private reads lead to a shared card and clauses you can both sign.

    Requires both under-pressure reads. Each question shows what the other person will see. Including further-read patterns needs a separate mutual yes.

Our approach

A reading.
Room for the real person.

Use the interpretation alongside your experience. Keep your words, the guidance, and missing context distinct.

Question the reading.

You can correct your Profile. Deep Read uses fixed, answer-linked text. Treat interpretations as starting points.

Know whose words they are.

Chosen answers, your authored lines, and interpreted patterns are labeled separately in your Guide.

Review what is shared.

Review whole-Guide sharing, links, and pair agreements in your account. See the current policy for visibility and withdrawal rules.

Control what Thea remembers.

Memory is on by default. Turning it off stops new remembering; it does not delete existing information. Conversation history and learned information have separate deletion controls.

Keep the limits in view.

Interpretations can be incomplete or wrong. Use readings for development and collaboration, not hiring, pay, promotion, or discipline.
