Who we are

Understanding becomes useful
between people.

SuperQ is an AI coach for leaders and their teams. We reveal how you and your people are built, how you think, decide, communicate, and handle pressure, then coach you to turn that into how you actually work together. Every day. In the moments that count.

The unit of value isn't your profile. It's the relationship: you and your co-founder, you and your new COO, your team as a whole. SuperQ is where knowing yourself becomes leading others, and leading others becomes results.

Why we built it

We built SuperQ because the hardest problems in building a company are never the ones on the spreadsheet.

Mark scaled ZICO Coconut Water from nothing to a Coca-Cola acquisition, then bought it back. He'd invested in and sat on the boards of hundreds of companies. Over and over, the ones that struggled, and the ones that broke, didn't fail for lack of talent or capital. They failed because of this: "Why can't my co-founder and I see this the same way?" "Why does this brilliant hire just not fit?" "Why do we keep having the same argument in different costumes?"

He'd done the assessments. Worked with world-class coaches. Read the books. None of it gave him what he actually needed: a clear, practical guide for how I'm wired, how you're wired, and how we interact in the moments that count.

Marcus had spent a decade building AI systems and coaching entrepreneurs to understand themselves. He saw the same gap from the other side: tools that were deep on insight and shallow on action. He wanted something that could see people with real precision and turn that into practical guidance for how they actually work together. So he built it.

SuperQ is the tool we both spent years wishing existed.

What we believe

Five things we hold to be true.

  1. Precision beats insight.

    Knowing your "type" is interesting. Understanding how your approach differs from your new COO’s, and what to discuss, is useful. We built SuperQ for the useful.

  2. The relationship is the unit.

    Most tools stop at the individual. The real leverage is in the space between people: co-founder dynamics, exec alignment, team composition. That's where companies are won and lost.

  3. Self-knowledge is a competitive advantage.

    Every leader knows their biggest lever is themselves. Knowing how you're wired, how you decide, communicate, and show up under pressure, is the foundation everything else is built on.

  4. Meet people where they are.

    You can't unlock someone's potential by pushing them into your mold. The most effective leaders see how each person is actually wired and meet them there. That's not soft. That's a strategic advantage.

  5. The version of you that scales is the real you.

    When you stop performing the role and start leading as who you actually are, your team gives you the same permission. That's when everything accelerates.

The founders

Built by people
who lived the problem.

Co-founder

Mark Rampolla

Mark has spent his career building what didn't yet exist. He began in technology at IBM before joining the Peace Corps, then went on to lead a 300-person business across Latin America for a Fortune 50 company. He founded ZICO Coconut Water, growing it from an idea into a category-creating brand that was acquired by The Coca-Cola Company. Along the way, ZICO helped create an $8 billion global industry and 200,000 jobs. Years later, Mark bought the company back and is now building it toward $1B+.

Over the last two decades, Mark has invested in and advised more than 100 entrepreneurs. Today, he leads Ground Force Capital, a $600 million investment firm partnering with founders, CEOs, and executive teams to build enduring companies. He is also the bestselling author of High-Hanging Fruit and An Entrepreneur's Guide to Freedom.

Across every company he built, invested in, or served, Mark kept discovering the same truth: the biggest challenges were never strategy, capital, or execution. They were human. Fear. Identity. Communication. Trust. SuperQ is the tool he spent the last 20 years wishing existed.

Co-founder

Marcus Bell

Marcus is a builder of a different kind: polymath, multi-instrumentalist, music producer, coach, futurist, and technologist who has been deep in AI for over a decade.

He's spent his career at the intersection of AI and human potential: coaching hundreds of creators and entrepreneurs to understand how they're wired, and building the systems that make that kind of precision available at scale. He wanted a tool that could see people with real depth and turn that into practical, field-ready guidance for how they work together.

So he built one. Marcus designed the foundational SuperQ assessment engine and AI layer: the system that maps your wiring, overlays your key relationships, and delivers the exact scripts and protocols for the moments that matter. He believes AI's greatest contribution isn't automation. It's helping people finally see themselves, and each other, clearly enough to do something about it.
